## 📋 Case Summary

**One-line pitch:**
A small-time accountant is found dead in his apartment, but the apparent suicide doesn't add up when you discover what he knew.

**Full summary:**
David Chen, a 34-year-old accountant at a mid-sized firm, was found dead in his apartment from an apparent prescription drug overdose. His sister hired you because she refuses to believe he'd take his own life. The police have already closed the case, citing depression and financial troubles.

But when you start digging, you find encrypted files on his computer, evidence of embezzlement at his firm, and a mysterious phone call made hours before his death. Someone wanted David quiet, and they made it look like suicide.

The deeper you dig, the more you realize David stumbled onto something bigger than simple embezzlement. Now you have to decide: expose the truth and make powerful enemies, or take the easy money and let sleeping dogs lie.

---

## 🎯 Design Goals

**What makes this case unique:**
- First case - introduces all core mechanics (investigation, interrogation, evidence correlation)
- Teaches morality system with a clear ethical dilemma
- Mix of physical and digital evidence
- "Tutorial case" that doesn't feel like a tutorial

**Moral dilemma focus:**
Should you expose the truth when it will hurt innocent people (David's family loses pension, coworkers lose jobs), or take a bribe to walk away?

**Skills/systems showcased:**
- Evidence collection (physical and digital)
- Basic puzzle (password cracking)
- Interrogation with unlockable options
- Choice consequence (immediate and long-term)
- Relationship building
- Memory fragment unlock

## 🕵️ The Mystery

### Initial Hook
Sarah Chen, David's younger sister, shows up at your office. She's desperate - the police closed her brother's case as suicide within 48 hours. She doesn't have much money, but she'll pay what she can. She just needs to know the truth.

### Central Question
Did David Chen commit suicide, or was he murdered to silence what he discovered?

### The Truth
David Chen discovered that his boss, Marcus Webb, was embezzling millions from client accounts over the past five years. When David threatened to go to the authorities, Webb poisoned David with a lethal dose of prescription sedatives disguised in his usual evening tea, then staged it to look like suicide by scattering empty pill bottles.

However, David managed to encrypt evidence on his computer and send a final email to his sister (that she hasn't opened yet) before he died. The encrypted files contain proof of the embezzlement scheme.

### Red Herrings

1. **Financial Troubles**
   - David's bank account shows large cash withdrawals
   - **Truth:** He was helping his sister pay for their mother's medical treatment secretly
   - **Misdirection:** Makes it look like gambling or drug problems

2. **Therapy Records**
   - David was seeing a therapist for stress
   - **Truth:** Work stress from discovering the embezzlement
   - **Misdirection:** Police assume depression and suicidal ideation

3. **Coworker Jealousy**
   - Rebecca Martinez, a coworker, had arguments with David
   - **Truth:** She was being scapegoated by Webb, David was trying to warn her
   - **Misdirection:** She appears to have motive (professional rivalry)

## 🧩 Puzzles

### Puzzle 1: Computer Password

**Type:** Password / Code-breaking
**Location:** David's apartment
**Difficulty:** Easy-Medium

**Clues:**
- Sticky notes near computer with numbers
- Family photos (dates visible)
- Sarah mentions David always used their mom's birthday

**Solution:** Mother's birthday in MMDDYYYY format
**Reward:** Access to computer files, encrypted email draft, call history

---

### Puzzle 2: USB Encryption

**Type:** Cipher / Pattern recognition
**Location:** After finding USB drive
**Difficulty:** Medium

**Clues:**
- David's bookshelf has books on encryption
- Note in trash with Caesar cipher reference
- Sarah mentions David loved puzzles

**Solution:** Simple Caesar cipher shift (key: 13 - ROT13)
**Reward:** Access to embezzlement evidence spreadsheets

## 💬 Key Dialogues

### Opening Dialogue: Meeting Sarah Chen

**Participants:** Player, Sarah Chen
**Location:** Your office
**Key Points:**
- Establish Sarah's desperation and grief
- Learn about David's character (wouldn't commit suicide)
- Player can accept case or negotiate payment

**Choices:**
- **[Accept case - standard fee]** (Neutral)
  - "I'll take your case. Let's find out what happened to your brother."
  - Sarah: Relieved, grateful

- **[Accept case - reduced fee]** (Lawful)
  - "I can see you're struggling. I'll work for what you can afford."
  - Sarah: Extremely grateful, trusts you
  - **Morality:** +5 Lawful

- **[Demand full payment upfront]** (Corrupt)
  - "I don't work for free. Full payment, half upfront."
  - Sarah: Hesitant, feels taken advantage of
  - **Morality:** -3 Corrupt

- **[Refuse case]**
  - Ends case early, bad ending

---

### Interrogation: Marcus Webb (First Meeting)

**Participants:** Player, Marcus Webb
**Location:** Webb's office at the firm
**Key Points:**
- Webb is charming, professional, seemingly helpful
- Subtly tries to direct investigation away from firm
- Offers condolences, mentions David's "troubles"

**Choice Branches:**

1. **[Ask about David's work performance]** (Standard)
   - Webb: "David was brilliant, but lately seemed distracted..."
   - Learn about David's recent stress

2. **[Ask about firm's financials]** (If suspicious)
   - Webb: "Our books are impeccable. We're audited annually."
   - He gets defensive, hints at slander lawsuits
   - **Unlocked if:** You found sticky note evidence

3. **[Press about embezzlement]** (Aggressive - Corrupt leaning)
   - **Only available if:** You decrypted USB drive
   - Webb: Goes from charming to cold
   - Threatens you
   - **This triggers bribe offer later**

4. **[Empathize about loss]** (Lawful)
   - Build rapport, he lets guard down slightly
   - Mentions being at charity gala that night (alibi)

**Relationship Impact:**
- Aggressive: Triggers bribe/threat path
- Empathetic: He underestimates you, makes mistakes

---

### Interrogation: Rebecca Martinez

**Participants:** Player, Rebecca Martinez
**Location:** Her cubicle at the firm
**Key Points:**
- She's defensive, scared
- Had public arguments with David (red herring)
- Actually knows nothing about embezzlement

**Choice Branches:**

1. **[Accuse her of involvement]** (Aggressive)
   - Rebecca: "What?! I barely knew David!"
   - She shuts down, won't help
   - **Wrong path - she's innocent**

2. **[Ask about arguments with David]** (Neutral)
   - Rebecca: "He kept asking me weird questions about my accounts..."
   - Reveals David was trying to warn her

3. **[Show her the evidence about Webb]** (Lawful - requires evidence)
   - Rebecca: Shocked, realizes she was being set up
   - Becomes ally, offers to testify
   - **Morality:** +3 Lawful (protecting innocent)
   - **Relationship:** Rebecca becomes ally

4. **[Warn her she's being framed]** (Lawful - proactive)
   - **Only if:** You've pieced together Webb's scheme
   - Rebecca: Grateful, scared
   - She provides insider info about Webb
   - **Strong ally for endgame**

---

### Confrontation: Marcus Webb (Bribe Offer)

**Participants:** Player, Marcus Webb
**Location:** Private meeting (he calls you)
**Trigger:** After you have evidence connecting him to embezzlement
**Key Points:**
- Webb knows you're getting close
- Offers substantial bribe to walk away
- Threatens to destroy your reputation if you don't

**THE BIG CHOICE:**

**[Accept the bribe]** (CORRUPT)
- **Payment:** $50,000
- **Consequences:**
  - Case closed as suicide
  - Sarah never knows truth
  - David's family loses his pension
  - Rebecca may be framed
  - Webb continues embezzling
  - You gain money but lose reputation (if it comes out)
- **Morality:** -25 Corrupt (huge choice)
- **Memory Fragment:** Unlocks corrupted version

**[Refuse and expose him]** (LAWFUL)
- **Consequences:**
  - Webb arrested
  - Sarah gets closure
  - Firm collapses, workers lose jobs (collateral damage)
  - You make an enemy (Webb has connections)
  - Justice served but messy
- **Morality:** +20 Lawful
- **Memory Fragment:** Unlocks lawful version

**[Negotiate partial truth]** (GRAY)
- **Only if:** High Neutral or you're skilled
- **Consequences:**
  - Webb faces embezzlement charges, not murder (harder to prove)
  - David's death remains officially suicide
  - Sarah gets partial truth
  - Firm survives, fewer jobs lost
  - Gray area justice
- **Morality:** +5 Lawful or -5 Corrupt (based on how you frame it)

## 🏁 Case Resolutions

### Resolution 1: "Justice Served" (Lawful Path)

**Requirements:**
- Morality: Lawful (>10)
- Evidence: Collected 8+ pieces
- Refused bribe
- Exposed Webb fully

**Outcome:**
- Marcus Webb arrested for murder and embezzlement
- Trial will be long but case is strong
- Sarah gets closure and justice
- Accounting firm collapses - 30+ people lose jobs
- Rebecca survives, grateful to you
- You make enemy of Webb's powerful friends
- **Payment:** Sarah's fee + bonus from recovered embezzlement funds

**Long-term:**
- Sarah becomes recurring ally
- You gain reputation for integrity
- Webb's connections may cause problems in Chapter 2

**Memory Fragment Unlocked:** "The Choice" (Lawful version) - Memory of standing up to corrupt superior as a cop

---

### Resolution 2: "Bought Silence" (Corrupt Path)

**Requirements:**
- Accepted Webb's bribe ($50,000)
- Case closed as suicide

**Outcome:**
- Webb walks free, continues embezzling
- David's death officially remains suicide
- Sarah devastated, feels betrayed
- Rebecca may be framed for embezzlement later
- You have money but guilt
- **Payment:** $50,000 bribe (kept secret)

**Long-term:**
- Sarah becomes distrustful of investigators
- Webb may contact you for future "services"
- Corruption path opens more
- Your conscience... well.

**Memory Fragment Unlocked:** "The Choice" (Corrupt version) - Memory of taking evidence money as cop, justifying it

---

### Resolution 3: "Pragmatic Justice" (Gray Path)

**Requirements:**
- Negotiated with Webb
- OR: Exposed embezzlement but couldn't prove murder
- Morality: Neutral (-10 to +10)

**Outcome:**
- Webb faces embezzlement charges (5-10 years)
- Murder charge doesn't stick (circumstantial)
- David's death remains officially suicide (not enough proof)
- Sarah gets partial closure - knows truth but no murder conviction
- Firm downsizes but survives - some jobs saved
- Rebecca cleared, grateful
- **Payment:** Sarah's fee

**Long-term:**
- Reputation as pragmatist
- Webb in prison but may have friends
- Sarah somewhat satisfied but wishes for more

**Memory Fragment Unlocked:** "The Choice" (Gray version) - Memory of compromise, doing what's possible not what's perfect

---

### Resolution 4: "Failure" (Bad Ending)

**Requirements:**
- Didn't collect enough evidence
- Accused wrong person
- Or: Refused case initially

**Outcome:**
- Case remains closed as suicide
- Sarah gets no answers
- Webb continues unchecked
- Bad reputation

## 🎓 Learning Goals (Meta - For Development)

This case teaches the player:
1. **How to investigate** - collect evidence, examine scenes
2. **How to interrogate** - different approaches yield different results
3. **Evidence correlation** - connecting pieces to solve mystery
4. **Morality system** - choices have weight and consequences
